## Locked decisions
1. Release the introductory handbook free of charge. Do not place it behind checkout, email collection, or PayPal.
2. Maintain the public GitHub repository as the source for editable templates, change history, and community contributions.
3. Provide a downloadable PDF, accessible interactive HTML edition with working copy controls, and standalone audit templates.
4. Feature the publication in the Reasoning and Logic catalogue as a Free Technical Guide. As a newly published title it must appear first when released.
5. Give it a dedicated public landing page that explains the contents and demonstrates a working sample prompt. Offer clear actions: Read Online, Download PDF, View on GitHub.
6. Retain the illustrated cover, exact US Letter PDF page size, and the established interior publishing layout standards. Do not replace the cover with the geometric SVG proposal.
7. Attribute discreetly as J. Nasr. Do not use a repeated name footer or the label "Credit:". Remove generic AI marketing phrasing and avoid unnecessary dash punctuation.
8. Treat any future paid toolkit as a separate product that requires independently verified functionality. Do not monetize the current introductory handbook.
9. Keep the bookstore's canonical product identity separate from edition, version, price, checkout identity, and URL. No checkout is required for this free guide.
10. No locked bookstore publication, live storefront, payments, or releases may be changed as an incidental part of preparing this guide.

## Landing page brief
Audience: people building websites or apps with AI who want a practical way to check the output, especially beginners.

Hero:
Title: Vibe Coding Safety Check
Supporting copy: A free guide with 22 checks for code quality, dependencies, security, performance, and accessibility in AI-built websites and apps.
Primary action: Read the Guide
Secondary actions: Download PDF; View on GitHub

Below the hero:
- What the guide checks, in six concise groups
- Three-step use: choose a check, copy a prompt, review evidence
- Interactive sample prompt with functional Copy control
- Honest boundaries: not a substitute for professional security review, a successful scan is not a guarantee of safety
- Download formats and links
- Brief FAQ about tool access, execution versus simulated reviews, and approvals

Visual direction:
- Match bookstore cover-display rules: bounded zone, preserved aspect ratio, no crop or stretch.
- Show the actual illustrated cover, not a stock device image or SVG replacement.
- Clean typography and controlled spacing with responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts.
- Avoid exaggerated promises, fabricated scores, chat artifacts, and generic AI marketing wording.
